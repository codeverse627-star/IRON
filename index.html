Why IronPulse

More than a gym.
A standard.

We started in a 900 sq ft garage with three barbells and one stubborn belief: anyone can get stronger with the right coaching. Twelve years later we're Riverside's largest independent strength club — same belief, bigger room.

  • ✓
    Coach-led, never cookie-cutter. Every block is programmed, tracked and adjusted for you.
  • ✓
    Capped classes. A maximum of 14 people means you actually get coached.
  • ✓
    Recovery suite. Sauna, cold plunge and a mobility lab to keep you training.
  • ✓
    No lock-in. Month-to-month memberships. Cancel anytime, no games.
